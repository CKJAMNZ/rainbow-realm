# 🌈 Rainbow Realm

Bella 💗 and Ralph 💙 race around a sparkly rainbow world collecting gems.
The first to collect **10 gems** wins!

**Play:** https://ckjamnz.github.io/rainbow-realm/

## How to play
- **Bella:** W A S D keys, or the pink buttons (bottom left)
- **Ralph:** arrow keys, or the blue buttons (bottom right)
- Two players on one iPad or computer. Tap **Play!** to start.

Made with one HTML file, a canvas and plain JavaScript.
